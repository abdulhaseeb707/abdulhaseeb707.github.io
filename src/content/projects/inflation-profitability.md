## Why I asked it

Inflation is one of the first things people blame when a business has a hard year. Some firms can pass rising
costs on to their customers. Others cannot, and their margins shrink. I wanted to look at the numbers instead
of assuming one story or the other.

## What I did

I put together company financial figures alongside inflation, measured each firm's profitability, and set
out the hypotheses I wanted to test.

I then estimated multiple regression models in Stata, checked whether the relationships I expected actually
showed up in the data, and interpreted the results in economic terms: what they mean for how businesses plan,
and what they suggest about why price stability matters.
